# 💖 A Magical Birthday Surprise for Deepali

A premium, romantic, interactive birthday website crafted with love for **Deepali** from **Govind**.

---

## ✨ Features & Experiences

1. **Dreamy Romantic Aesthetics & Visual Theme**:
   - Palette of blush pink, deep red, soft lavender, white, and champagne gold.
   - Canvas-based particle background with drifting rose petals, twinkling golden stars, floating glowing hearts, and mouse interactive lighting.
   - Google Fonts: *Great Vibes*, *Playfair Display*, and *Outfit*.
   - Glassmorphism cards with smooth blur and glowing shadows.

2. **Full-Screen Opening Screen**:
   - Heading: *"Happy Birthday, My Deepali ❤️"*
   - Subtitle: *"Today is not just your birthday; it's a celebration of the beautiful person who makes my world special."*
   - Glowing animated button: *"Open Your Surprise 🎁"* with sparkle burst.

3. **Two Personal Photos & Precious Memory Vault**:
   - **Photo 1**: Ornate glowing frame with floating heart badges and caption: *"One picture, a thousand beautiful feelings ❤️"*.
   - **Photo 2**: Authentic Polaroid frame with real-time 3D tilt tracking, washi tape, and handwritten caption: *"My favourite memories have you in them 💗"*.
   - **Precious Memory Vault**: Expandable gallery revealing all 14 personal photos from college days, candid smiles, and sweet moments together, with full-screen zoom modal.

4. **Interactive Birthday Cake**:
   - Layered birthday cake with strawberry frosting, sugar pearls, and glowing candles with flickering flames.
   - Button: *"Make a Wish ✨"*.
   - Candles blow out with rising smoke wisps, ambient lighting dims, full-screen confetti cannon explodes, revealing:
     > *"May your smile always shine brighter than every star in the sky."*
   - Includes a *"Light Candles Again 🕯️"* option.

5. **Our Little Love Story**:
   - Centerpiece quote card:
     > *"Some people make ordinary days feel extraordinary. You are one of those rare people who make the world feel warmer, brighter, and more beautiful just by being themselves."*
   - Romantic journey milestone cards with scroll reveals and glowing accents.

6. **A Secret Love Letter**:
   - Vintage envelope sealed with a 3D wax seal monogrammed **"G • D"**.
   - Clicking breaks the wax seal with sparkles and reveals the letter line by line with a typewriter animation and floating hearts:
     > *"Dear Deepali,  
     > Happy Birthday to someone truly special. ❤️  
     > I hope this new chapter of your life brings you happiness, peace, beautiful surprises, and countless reasons to smile. You deserve all the love and kindness that life has to offer.  
     > May your dreams come closer, your worries become lighter, and your days become brighter.  
     > Today, I just want you to feel celebrated and appreciated for the wonderful person you are.  
     > Happy Birthday, Deepali! 💗  
     > With lots of love,  
     > Govind ❤️"*

7. **Romantic Background Music Player**:
   - Starts automatically upon interacting with *"Open Your Surprise 🎁"*.
   - Floating music widget with animated sound equalizer bars, play/pause, and mute controls.
   - Audio file located at `public/romantic-music.mp3`.

8. **Grand Finale & Replay**:
   - Full-screen celebration with animated fireworks, sparkling stars, pulsing hearts, and the message:
     > *"Happy Birthday, Deepali! 🎂❤️  
     > Keep smiling, keep shining, and never forget how special you are."*
   - *"Replay Our Little Surprise 💕"* button smoothly scrolls back to the beginning to experience the magic again.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18+) is installed.

### Steps
1. Open a terminal in the project folder:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```
2. Start the development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:3000
   ```

## 🌐 How to Deploy to Vercel or Netlify

### Deploying to Vercel
1. Install Vercel CLI (or connect via GitHub):
   ```bash
   npx vercel
   ```
2. Follow the on-screen prompts. Vercel automatically detects Vite!
3. Build command: `npm run build`
4. Output directory: `dist`

### Deploying to Netlify
1. Drag and drop the `dist` folder directly into [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Or use Netlify CLI:
   ```bash
   npx netlify deploy --prod --dir=dist
   ```
